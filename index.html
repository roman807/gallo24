Gallo24

Gallo24 is a personal AI chat assistant, built and used by a single individual developer for their own work. It combines locally-run language models (via Ollama, running on the developer's own computer) with Infomaniak's Swiss-hosted AI API, and can optionally search a specific, individually-designated Google Drive folder to answer questions about the developer's own documents.

Gallo24 is not a public product or service — it has exactly one user (its developer) and is not distributed, sold, or made available to anyone else.
