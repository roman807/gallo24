Gallo24 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Gallo24 is a personal project with a single user: its developer. This policy describes how it handles data for that one account — it is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

What Gallo24 accesses

With explicit authorization via Google Sign-In, Gallo24 requests read-only access to Google Drive (the drive.readonly scope). The application itself further restricts this to a single, specific folder (and its subfolders) that the developer has designated — it never reads, lists, or otherwise accesses any other file or folder in the Drive account, even though the underlying Google permission is broader. Gallo24 never writes to, modifies, or deletes anything in Google Drive.

How that data is used

Content from the designated folder is used only to answer the developer's own questions inside the app — for example, searching for a document or asking about its contents. This happens in two ways:

  • Processed locally, on the developer's own computer, by a locally-running language model; or
  • Sent to Infomaniak's AI API (a Swiss company, processing and storing data exclusively within Switzerland) for either generating a response or computing a search embedding.

No other third party receives this data. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than directly answering the developer's own request.

Where data is stored

Chat history and a local search index derived from the designated Drive folder are stored locally, on the developer's own computer. Infomaniak's stated policy is that customer data is used solely to provide the service, is not used to train models, and is not shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.
